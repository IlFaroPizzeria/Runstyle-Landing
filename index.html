The problem

Every run starts with the same doubt.

Long sleeves or short?
Wind vest or not?
Cold at the start or hot after mile 3?
And how long will today's session actually take?

Most runners figure this out by instinct, checking a generic weather app that has no idea what clothes you own or how your body reacts. RunStylist doesn't just tell you the forecast — it tells you what to wear, with what you already have, and how long your full workout will take.

How it works

Three steps, before every run.

01

Photograph your gear

The AI recognizes garment type, material, and sleeve length. You confirm, and it's saved to your digital wardrobe — a one-time setup.

02

Describe your workout

Where you're running, what time, the type of session, and, if you want, its full structure: reps, paces, rest.

03

Get your outfit and duration

RunStylist cross-checks your wardrobe with the exact weather and calculates what to wear and how long it'll take — before you walk out the door.

FAQ

Before you ask.

Do I need a lot of technical gear for this to work?+

No. It works with whatever you already own, even 4 or 5 pieces. The more you add, the more precise the combinations get.

How does it recognize my clothes from a photo?+

It analyzes the image and detects garment type, sleeve length, and likely material. You confirm or correct the details before it's saved.

How does it calculate my workout duration?+

It uses the structure you enter — reps, distances, rest, warm-up, and cool-down — along with a reference of your recent level, to estimate the real total time of the session.

What if I don't own the ideal piece for today's weather?+

It recommends the best possible combination with what you have, and flags gaps that keep coming up.

Does it connect with Garmin, Strava, or Apple Health?+

It's on the roadmap. The beta focuses first on the wardrobe and the recommendation; integrations come in later phases.

Is this a training or health app?+

No. RunStylist doesn't plan workouts or give medical advice — it focuses on what to wear and how long you'll be out there.

When will it be available?+

We're in the validation and beta-building phase. Whoever joins the waitlist will be among the first to try it.
